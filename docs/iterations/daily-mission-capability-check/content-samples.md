# Pilot Content

| Day | Capability | Mission | Required ideas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduce yourself | Say your name, where you are from, and what you do. | Name; place; work or study |
| 24 | Buy simple things | Say what you want, ask for it, and make your choice. | Item; request; choice |
| 26 | Ask for help | Explain a problem, ask for help, and check the answer. | Problem; request; check or answer |

## Day 1 Example

- Listening: `My name is Anna. I am from China. I am a student.`
- Question: `What does Anna do?`
- Learner output: `My name is Li. I am from China. I am a student.`
- Optional support: keywords, sentence starters, and one reference answer.

All text is defined in `src/content/capabilityChecks.ts`. Content validation requires stable IDs, a correct listening option, three or more required ideas, and at least three sentences for each reference answer.
